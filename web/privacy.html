Privacy Policy

GATE CSE 2027 Command Center · Last updated: 2 October 2026

This app is a personal study tracker for GATE CSE aspirants, made by Abhinav. This page explains what data it handles. It is written in plain language and is not legal advice.

What the app stores

  • Your study data: syllabus progress, study-time logs, revision queue, mock tests and scores, settings and schedule. This is kept on your device (browser or app storage).
  • If you create a sync account: your email address, your password (stored only as a hash by the authentication service, never readable by me), and a copy of your study data so your devices can stay in sync.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party trackers.
  • Your data is never sold or shared for marketing.
  • Without a sync account, nothing leaves your device.

Where sync data lives

Sync data is stored with Supabase, which provides the database and sign-in service. Access is restricted per user: each account can only read and write its own rows. Data travels over HTTPS.

How your email is used

Only for signing in, and (if enabled) for confirmation or password-reset messages. It is not used for newsletters or promotion.

Deleting your data

  • Delete account (Today tab → Sync): permanently deletes your account and all synced data from the server. Data already on your devices stays there until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.
  • You can also export your data at any time (Today tab → Backup → Export data).
  • If you cannot use the in-app option, email me at the address below and I will delete your account.

Retention

Synced data is kept until you delete your account. Encrypted database backups may be kept for up to 30 days after deletion.

Children

The app is intended for exam aspirants and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
